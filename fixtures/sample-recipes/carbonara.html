Classic Spaghetti Carbonara

Italian Cooking Magazine
Serves 4
15 minutes
20 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spaghetti
  • 8 oz guanciale or pancetta, diced
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup freshly grated Pecorino Romano
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Kosher salt

Directions

Bring a large pot of salted water to boil. Cook spaghetti according to package directions until al dente.

While pasta cooks, heat a large skillet over medium heat. Add guanciale and cook until crispy, about 8 minutes.

In a bowl, whisk together eggs, Pecorino, Parmesan, and plenty of black pepper.

Reserve 1 cup pasta water, then drain pasta. Working quickly, add hot pasta to the skillet with guanciale.

Remove from heat and immediately add egg mixture, tossing constantly. Add pasta water as needed to create a creamy sauce.

Serve immediately with extra cheese and pepper.

Notes

The key to this dish is to work quickly and use the residual heat from the pasta to cook the eggs without scrambling them.
