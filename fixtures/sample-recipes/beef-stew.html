Slow Cooker Beef Stew

Comfort Cooking
8 servings
20 minutes
8 hours
  • 2 lbs beef stew meat, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1/4 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 4 cups beef broth
  • 1 lb baby potatoes, halved
  • 4 carrots, peeled and cut into chunks
  • 2 stalks celery, sliced
  • 1 large onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 teaspoon dried rosemary
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 cup frozen peas
  1. Toss beef cubes with flour, salt, and pepper in a large bowl.
  2. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Brown beef in batches, about 3 minutes per side. Transfer to slow cooker.
  3. Add potatoes, carrots, celery, onion, and garlic to the slow cooker.
  4. Whisk together beef broth, tomato paste, thyme, and rosemary. Pour over beef and vegetables.
  5. Add bay leaves and stir gently to combine.
  6. Cover and cook on LOW for 8-10 hours or HIGH for 4-5 hours, until beef is tender.
  7. Add frozen peas in the last 30 minutes of cooking.
  8. Remove bay leaves before serving. Adjust seasoning as needed.

This stew tastes even better the next day! Store in the refrigerator for up to 4 days or freeze for up to 3 months.
