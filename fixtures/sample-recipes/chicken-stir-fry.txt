Easy Chicken Stir Fry

Ingredients:
1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, sliced
2 tablespoons vegetable oil
3 cups mixed vegetables (broccoli, bell peppers, snap peas)
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 tablespoon fresh ginger, minced
1/4 cup soy sauce
2 tablespoons honey
1 tablespoon sesame oil
1 tablespoon cornstarch
Red pepper flakes to taste
Cooked rice for serving

Directions:
Whisk together soy sauce, honey, sesame oil, and cornstarch in a small bowl. Set aside.
Heat vegetable oil in a large wok or skillet over high heat.
Add chicken and stir fry until cooked through, about 5-6 minutes. Remove and set aside.
Add more oil if needed, then add vegetables. Stir fry for 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender.
Add garlic and ginger, cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
Return chicken to the pan and pour sauce over everything.
Stir fry 1-2 minutes until sauce thickens and coats everything.
Serve immediately over rice, garnished with red pepper flakes.

Source: Mom's Kitchen
Prep Time: 15 minutes
Cook Time: 15 minutes
Servings: 4

Categories: Asian, Chinese, Chicken, Quick & Easy, Weeknight
